Brussels achieves agreement to end shark finning

ICCAT members, including the EU, reached an agreement to end shark finning by requiring sharks to be landed with fins naturally attached. The rules will be implemented in phases starting in 2027 for fresh sharks and 2030 for frozen sharks.
